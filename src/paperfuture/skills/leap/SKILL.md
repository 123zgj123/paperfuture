# Mechanism-centered research design · v1

Use the development graph to locate a persistent bottleneck and the assumption that sustains it. Consider whether removing a constraint, reversing a dependence, reframing the target or transferring a causal principle could address that bottleneck. Choose one intervention and explain its observable consequences.

For a transfer across fields, map the source and target variables, conditions and failure boundaries. Shared terminology or attaching an alignment component to a security pipeline is insufficient: state the interaction or principle that changes, and a control that isolates its effect.

Inspect the closest existing mechanism and a plausible counterexample with the provided source tools. Present a concise research rationale, causal hypothesis, minimal experiment and falsification rule. Do not fabricate failed experiments, breakthrough narratives or private reasoning traces. The controller permits one candidate per round; this procedure cannot create hidden alternatives or extra candidate rounds.
