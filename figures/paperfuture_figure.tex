  \caption{PaperFuture framework.
  (1--2) Year-stratified, versioned literature emphasizes the latest two calendar
  years and is selected for relevance and trajectory coverage,
  then organized into a directed graph of research stages with evidence-backed
  technical transitions. Independent LLM review checks relations and scores
  technical change on five 0--10 dimensions and an overall 0--100 scale.
  Edge widths and numbers illustrate this magnitude, not observed measurements.
  (3--5) An LLM proposes a leap using the global stage graph and detailed evidence.
  Multiple API models independently simulate conditional outcomes and critiques,
  discuss disagreements and assess value and feasibility. Fixed decision rules
  either stop exploration or use cumulative predictions, bottlenecks and retained
  assumptions to retrieve evidence and locate the next leap.
  All candidates share a global limit of four exploration rounds.
  (6) The best assessed candidate is returned with its value, feasibility,
  uncertainty and validation plan. Dashed arrows carry simulated feedback;
  these predictions are never promoted to facts in the historical graph.
  All model inference uses APIs. Software execution does not establish validated
  scientific forecasting performance.}
  \label{fig:paperfuture-framework}
